Selling on Amazon Japan

By Shashi Kant · Selling channels

Editorial guide published by Shashi Kant. Platform names identify the services discussed. Consult the linked providers for their official terms.

How do I estimate the cost of selling on Amazon Japan?

Combine the selling plan, category referral fee, fulfilment costs, storage and returns. Then subtract acquisition costs when calculating how much contribution remains.

  • Look up the fee base and band for the exact offer.
  • Model shipping with measured package dimensions and verify charges against settlement.

Which offer is worth launching on Amazon Japan?

Launch when a small set of products can win an identifiable customer search, stay in stock and earn contribution after referral charges, shipping, returns and advertising. Start by validating a single listing and its actual settlement before multiplying colours, pack sizes and inventory. Amazon's public Japan pricing page lists ¥100 per item sold for the individual plan and ¥4,900 per month excluding tax for the professional plan, before other charges. The two listed plan charges equal each other at 49 items when compared on the same tax basis. That comparison covers the plan charges only, before category, fulfilment and other costs. Compare feature access and tax treatment before letting the arithmetic choose a plan. Read the live fee table for your product category and fee base Amazon Japan: seller pricing and FBA costs.

Decide who owns each Amazon offer

An Amazon offer joins a catalogue, a seller, an item condition, a price and a delivery promise. Confirm whether the product already has an appropriate catalogue entry. Check identifiers, pack quantity, model, ingredients or materials and variant attributes against what ships. Creating an unnecessary second listing for the same item can split your operational history, while joining the wrong listing gives the customer a product that does not match the detail page. Document evidence for barcode and brand rights and assign one person authority to request catalogue fixes.

Separate the product detail page from the economics of your offer. A beautiful image cannot correct a mismatch between a two-pack photograph and a one-pack price. An attractive price cannot compensate for a delivery promise the warehouse cannot meet. Before ads, ask two native readers to explain what arrives, who the product is for and how to choose the variant after looking at the mobile first screen. Compare their answers to the cartons your warehouse will actually ship.

Build a contribution model with exact fee bases

Amazon publishes category-specific referral fees and size-dependent FBA charges. Use the total sales price and the relevant rate band as the platform defines them, including any applicable category minimum. The relevant bands in its pricing table refer to ¥750 or less where applicable. Never infer that a single threshold is universal or that the result of multiplying is your exact invoice. Check category, total sales price definition, minimum, tax and rounding in the actual account Amazon Japan: seller pricing and FBA costs.

A one-yen change can cross a tier. In a purely illustrative category with 5% at ¥750 and 15.4% at ¥751, the modeled referral amounts are ¥37.50 and approximately ¥115.65 when displayed to two decimal places. Before other costs, the higher price leaves about ¥77.15 less under those assumptions. These decimals show the formula and do not represent how Amazon invoices fractional yen. A product paying ¥38 before a price change might need considerably more than a one-yen increase to restore the original margin. Verify live rates, rounding and the actual product classification before changing a catalogue.

Choose fulfilment per SKU rather than per brand

Compare FBA and merchant fulfilment at the same delivered promise. For FBA, enter measured packaged dimensions and weight and add inbound freight, storage, aged stock risk, removals and preparation. For merchant fulfilment, enter packaging, labour, carrier, resends, tracking and the coverage needed to respond when delivery goes wrong. Store actual measurements with the SKU. A redesigned box can change a model even when the product cost is unchanged.

Pilot the fulfilment method with enough inventory to observe stable fulfilment and a return. Simulate out-of-stock rules, audit saleable versus damaged returns and use a simple reconciliation: opening saleable stock plus confirmed inbound minus shipped units plus saleable returns minus damage and transfers equals closing saleable stock. A mismatch is an operational issue before it becomes a marketing problem.

Use ads to investigate searches

Separate brand queries from the problem or product queries you hope to acquire. A shopper looking for your own exact brand may convert at a low cost without teaching you how to reach someone new. Put high intent product terms in a separate cohort and look at query relevance, plausible price band and stock availability. Use ad reports to see what was attributed to spend and settlement records to see what you actually earned. Report the figures side by side.

Suppose a ¥3,000 order contributes ¥1,050 before ads, and the minimum desired contribution after ads is ¥500. The initial maximum advertising cost per incremental order is ¥550. If the team assumes a click-to-order rate of 5%, the corresponding maximum cost per click is about ¥27.50. At 2.5%, it falls to ¥13.75. A reported attributed order is not proof of an incremental order. If a test cannot distinguish branded demand from demand you created, lower the budget or describe the result as inconclusive.

Resolve returns and stock before you scale

Review actual reasons for returns in plain categories: misleading size or fit, damage in transit, manufacturing defect, changed mind or missing component. Changes to pictures and instructions can reduce fit errors, whereas a supplier fix may be required for defective units. When one batch produces disproportionate problems, isolate that inventory and preserve the tracking identifiers before merging the data with other weeks. A refund appears after the original sale, so compare matured order cohorts.

Monitor units available, days of cover, inbound timing and contribution together. In this inventory illustration, 200 saleable units at 10 orders a day give 20 days of cover. With a 16-day replenishment lead time and a week of safety cover, the modeled reorder trigger is 230 units, so the team is already late. Avoid bidding harder to sell inventory you cannot replace. If delivery estimates or listing eligibility change, record the date and exclude those days from direct before-and-after creative comparisons.

The operating sequence for a 90-day Amazon pilot

During days 1–30, approve a clean product specification, create the SKU economics model, validate the fee category and choose the trial fulfilment method. Place a real test order under legitimate platform rules and reconcile order, dispatch, customer messaging and settlement. Set a revenue target only after setting the minimum contribution, maximum cumulative loss and inventory cap.

During days 31–60, group search queries by intent and log each price, stock and creative change. Control spend when only a handful of returns have matured. If marketing wants to change price, headline and fulfilment in the same week, decide whether learning or immediate sales is the objective, since the test will no longer isolate one effect. Add the better converter as a portfolio SKU only when supply and quality checks pass.

During days 61–90, reconcile the test results to settlement and warehouse records. Separate weak listing quality from weak demand and insufficient evidence from a genuine failure. Give finance an option to expand, continue one more bounded test or stop and clear remaining stock. A positive media dashboard by itself cannot resolve deteriorating contribution or stock risk.

Decision exercises for your team

These scenarios are hypothetical. They do not describe actual clients, sellers or campaign results. Use the questions and sample inputs to review your own evidence, terms and operating limits.

Compare FBA and merchant fulfilment for a bulky SKU

Hypothetical scenario. An exercise-equipment seller has packed dimensions from the prototype but will add a protective end cap for production.

Repack an actual production unit and confirm its measured size and mass before entering the applicable fee bands. A contribution difference of ¥90 can vanish if the larger pack crosses a size band. Compare with self-fulfilment using a consistent delivery promise.

Prepare the review. To make this review possible, gather a product ID, packaged dimensions, a carrier quote covering multiple regions and an FBA estimate on the same date. Put the materials in the order the next reviewer will need them: the original evidence first, the interpretation second, and the decision third. Where two records disagree, keep both and identify who can supply the missing explanation.

For this case, send a trial batch only if the model still covers inbound handling and stock withdrawal. Identify the role that can stop the action and make sure the owner can see the evidence before the next commitment becomes irreversible.

Watch for this. A supplier spec describing the unboxed product cannot anchor an FBA cost estimate.

Validate a product identifier before listing

Hypothetical scenario. A nutrition accessory is packaged as singles and sets. Both are entered in a draft feed under the same commercial name.

Check the physical barcode and bundle count against the record that the existing detail page represents. A sale that ships one unit for a two-unit image is an offer integrity problem. Resolving it before the first order avoids contaminated return and marketing data.

Challenge the conclusion. Look for sample units, barcode evidence, packaging photos and the application or account response where a product identifier exception is used. Distinguish facts the team can observe today from assumptions it will test in the next period. Ask a reviewer outside the original implementation to challenge the strongest explanation. If the team cannot reconstruct the sequence, record the issue as unresolved.

The proposed decision rule is to freeze the offer until description, labels and warehouse picks agree. Record the earliest date the owner can make that decision and what activity should pause until the conditions are met.

Watch for this. A spreadsheet identifier copied across variants should be investigated rather than trusted.

Identify why reported ROAS improved

Hypothetical scenario. During a week of heavy branded query traffic, attributed revenue rose while order-level contribution fell.

Separate exact brand traffic, competitor terms and category discovery, then match settled orders to a comparable in-stock period. A cheap branded click can make a blended campaign look healthy. Validate the marginal channel before expanding bids on an untested category.

Keep the work reproducible. Use query report, discounts, aggregate sales and fees, refund cohorts and stock availability by day. Record which treatment applies, what changed during the observation and what part of the business was outside the scope. A dated snapshot lets another person compare the same conditions after the process or platform changes.

At the review, approve only the test budget that fits the contribution ceiling. Give the next owner a brief note on the decision, the facts that support it and one finding that would warrant reopening it.

Watch for this. Do not infer that each credited conversion was caused by that ad.

Reduce avoidable fit returns

Hypothetical scenario. A filter cartridge sells reliably, but shoppers are returning it for the wrong model.

Compare customer reasons with which model the listing, package and printed installation instructions actually support. An image update can address misunderstanding, but if the warehouse sends a different cartridge, a creative change will not solve the actual problem.

Open the review with evidence. Ask the responsible team to bring a return-reason sample by SKU, dated listing versions, compatibility evidence and warehouse pick records. Compare the record with what the buyer or operator actually experienced. Keep the original terms alongside any model so a new reviewer can distinguish differences in scope from errors in arithmetic or reporting.

For this case, fix the verified cause and measure results on the next group of orders after their return window. Identify the role that can stop the action and make sure the owner can see the evidence before the next commitment becomes irreversible.

Watch for this. Use redacted returns data and remove personal details from shared files.

Prepare a downside stock withdrawal

Hypothetical scenario. A limited seasonal set will no longer sell at full price when the season ends.

Set separate limits for age, daily markdown cost and inventory remaining before storage or withdrawal choices change. The decision to discount depends on the amount recoverable after advertising, fulfilment and the returns window. Compare with recovery by another route instead of treating the prior purchase price as an argument to continue.

Prepare the review. To make this review possible, gather payout forecast, permitted withdrawal options, days of cover and the cost to recover saleable stock. Put the materials in the order the next reviewer will need them: the original evidence first, the interpretation second, and the decision third. Where two records disagree, keep both and identify who can supply the missing explanation.

The proposed decision rule is to move or clear stock only within a written contribution and recovery policy. Record the earliest date the owner can make that decision and what activity should pause until the conditions are met.

Watch for this. Stock available in an inbound shipment cannot be sold twice across two channels.

Review a reseller’s access and ownership

Hypothetical scenario. A manufacturer proposes giving a reseller broad control over a family of product pages.

Document the seller of record, brand authorisation and what happens to advertising and catalogue access after a dispute. The business should be able to fix false information even if the reseller stops operating. A practical test checks the permissions necessary to fulfil this obligation.

Challenge the conclusion. Look for account roles, contracts, rights records, media account access and a written dispute escalation route. Distinguish facts the team can observe today from assumptions it will test in the next period. Ask a reviewer outside the original implementation to challenge the strongest explanation. If the team cannot reconstruct the sequence, record the issue as unresolved.

At the review, hold launch until there is a reversible way to manage listings and reconcile transactions. Give the next owner a brief note on the decision, the facts that support it and one finding that would warrant reopening it.

Watch for this. Do not grant administrative rights to solve an issue that only needs a limited role.

Separate fee performance from fulfilment performance

Hypothetical scenario. A brand has higher unit costs despite negotiating a better domestic carrier contract.

Reconcile the order mix, package dimensions, fulfilment method and storage charges over the same time window. A switch toward larger bundles could raise average cost even as the cost of delivering each SKU falls. Preserve per-SKU estimates before averaging the channel.

Keep the work reproducible. Use settlements, SKU margin extracts, inventory ageing, pick invoices and customer-paid shipping records. Record which treatment applies, what changed during the observation and what part of the business was outside the scope. A dated snapshot lets another person compare the same conditions after the process or platform changes.

For this case, change the operating process only after identifying whether the cost moved with price, mix or a genuine change in rates. Identify the role that can stop the action and make sure the owner can see the evidence before the next commitment becomes irreversible.

Watch for this. One blended number can hide a profitable high-volume SKU and a weak long-tail assortment.

Build the first settlement waterfall from orders

Reconcile each movement from the product price to actual cash on at least one normal order, a discounted order and a return. Write down item value, seller-funded discount, tax, shipping paid by the customer, platform fee amount, fulfilment, advertising allocation, adjustments and cash received. If net receipts already reflect a deduction, do not subtract the same cost twice. For management reporting, preserve the settlement classification and clearly identify any costs added from outside the platform, including warehousing or customer service.

Consider eighty orders, each with ¥3,000 net item sales before returns. Suppose a unit costs ¥1,100, referral is ¥300, fulfilment ¥420, inbound and packaging ¥170, fulfilment support and expected returns ¥160, and advertising allocation ¥350. The contribution is ¥500 per order, or ¥40,000 for eighty orders before fixed overhead, assuming the reserve adequately represents returns. A separate cohort of six returned items can inform the reserve. It should not be charged a second time against the same expected cost estimate without a reconciliation.

Now change only one input. If advertising rises to ¥500 per order, the contribution falls to ¥350. If a volume increase is of interest, calculate how many incremental units are needed to offset the ¥150 lost on the original eighty. Original contribution lost is ¥12,000. At ¥350 each, roughly 35 additional orders are needed to offset it before extra operating costs. Each incremental order also consumes stock and can add return handling. This is a decision model, not a fee forecast.

Audit listings and consumer intent as a paired exercise

Use a query such as a replacement item by manufacturer part number as one sample, then a broader use case as a second. The exact-number shopper may need assurance of compatibility and delivery. The broader shopper may need a concise selector that explains why the offer solves their problem. Record which question the listing actually answers by looking at its title, images and initial mobile display. Match the scope of claims to what is documented for the item.

Set a clear rule for returns tied to misunderstanding. If a meaningful share of new returns cite the same incompatible model, stop the corresponding keyword or clarify the offer before buying more traffic. A strong initial click rate can rise when the offer overstates general applicability. Improving accuracy may reduce clicks while improving retained orders and contribution. Report the intended mechanism and the figures you will watch at the next review.

Common questions about Amazon Japan fees and fulfilment

Does a public FBA estimate contain every cost? Use it to locate what it includes, then add any omissions that affect your product, such as inbound freight, packaging, long holding costs and handling exceptions. Compare with charges settled in your account once you have actual history.

Do I need the professional plan as soon as I reach 49 items? The 49-item comparison only matches quoted plan fees on a common tax basis. Consider required tools, listing eligibility, and sales volatility. Check the account's applicable tax and programme treatment before selecting.

Make account access part of the warehouse handoff

Name a storefront owner and a fulfilment owner, then give them written authority over the decisions they actually need to make. The support lead may need to see when an item shipped but may have no need to change banking or advertising settings. Test how someone on the next shift can identify a late order, report a warehouse mismatch and record the action taken. Maintain the user access list when roles change.

Include a contact for urgent catalogue errors and a procedure to pause advertising when saleable inventory is wrong. A seller who learns about a stock error from an advertising report has lost time to limit the operational impact.
