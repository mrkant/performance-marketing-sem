Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This policy explains how Shashi Kant (“I”, “me”) collects, uses, stores and protects personal information on the website shashikant.jp and related pages. It applies when you contact me through the contact form, email or similar means.

1. Information collected

When you use the contact form or email me, I may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Message content and any related business or role information you choose to share
  • Technical information such as IP address, browser type and access time (which may be recorded in server or hosting logs)

I do not intentionally use advertising cookies or tracking tools. Hosting or analytics services may keep minimal technical logs.

2. Purpose of use

Personal information is used only for:

  • Responding to your enquiry and related professional conversations
  • Providing information about requested services, advisory work, training or roles
  • Operating, securing and preventing abuse of the website
  • Meeting legal obligations where applicable

You are not added to a marketing mailing list by default, and your data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

3. Retention

Enquiry content is kept for as long as needed to fulfil the purpose of the conversation, then deleted or anonymised within a reasonable period. Longer retention may apply where required by law.

4. Third parties and cross-border processing

As a rule, personal information is not sold or provided to third parties for marketing. Processing may occur in these cases:

  • Service providers needed to run the site (hosting, email, form delivery), under appropriate contractual safeguards
  • Legal requests or obligations

Service providers may use servers outside Japan. By contacting me you acknowledge that such processing may occur.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to reduce unauthorised access, loss or alteration. No method of transmission over the internet is fully secure.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law (including Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information — APPI), you may request:

  • Access to personal information held about you
  • Correction or deletion
  • Cessation of use or restriction of processing

Send requests using the contact details below. A reasonable time will be taken to respond. Identity verification may be required.

7. Children

This site is not directed at minors. Personal information of children is not knowingly collected.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date on this page. Please check the latest version.
