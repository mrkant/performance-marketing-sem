Privacy notice

Shashi Kant operates Shashikant.jp. This notice explains how information is handled when you visit the site or send an enquiry.

Enquiries

If you use the contact form, it asks for your name, email address and message. I use this information to respond to your enquiry, continue the conversation you initiated and handle related requests.

The form sends your submission to Formspree for processing and delivery. Formspree describes processing in the United States and other countries where it operates. Read Formspree’s privacy policy. Please keep your message to the information needed for your enquiry and leave out identity documents, payment details, health records and other sensitive or confidential material.

Website operation and analytics

Hosting and security services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and requested pages to deliver and protect the site.

Some pages use Google Analytics to understand website usage. Google tags may process page addresses, interactions and device or browser information. Cookies or similar identifiers may be used, depending on the configuration. Google Tag Manager is used on some pages to load tags. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Retention and service providers

Enquiry information is kept for the time needed to respond and handle related requests, and for longer where required by law. Hosting, form delivery, email and analytics services may process information in providing their services.

Privacy requests

Use the Contact page and begin your message with “Privacy request” to ask how your information is handled or to request access, correction or other action under applicable law. You can also request information about the operator, including the address, or the measures used to safeguard retained personal data. Start with a brief description. If verification is needed, I will explain how to proceed.
