Privacy Policy

This policy explains how I, Shashi Kant, handle information on shashikant.jp.

When you contact me

I use the name, email address and message you provide to receive, manage and reply to your enquiry. If you email me directly, I use your email address and the information you include for the same purpose. Sending a message is optional. Please avoid sending passwords, identification documents, health information or other sensitive or confidential details.

The contact form uses Formspree to receive submissions, filter spam and send notifications to my site mailbox. Submissions can be stored in the Formspree archive, and copies can remain in email. Formspree describes processing in the United States and other locations in its privacy policy.

Website analytics

I use Google Analytics to understand visits and engagement and Microsoft Clarity to understand how people interact with pages through tools such as heatmaps and session recordings. Google Tag Manager loads site tags. Analytics tools can use cookies and receive information about pages visited, devices and site interactions. See Google’s privacy policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement.

Where you see the analytics banner, you can accept or reject optional analytics. Use Analytics preferences in the footer of those pages to change your choice. The banner saves your choice in your browser for up to 180 days. I am rolling out this control across the site. Pages still using the earlier analytics setup may load tracking before you make a choice. You can also manage cookies in your browser.

How long information is kept

Enquiry records may be held in the form archive and mailbox while the enquiry is being handled, and afterward where needed for follow-up, security, recordkeeping or a legal obligation. The exact period can vary with the circumstances. Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity have separate retention settings for their analytics data.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions or requests concerning your personal information, such as access, correction, deletion or stopping use, email contact@shashikant.jp. I may need enough information to verify the request and will respond according to applicable law.
