Business Opportunity Discovery

A structured methodology for turning market observation into prioritised, testable propositions. All opportunity statements remain hypotheses until validated.

Seven-stage methodology

Stage 1 — Observe the market ▼

Study category trends, product rankings, new launches, competitor movements and visible consumer behaviour. Prefer multi-year series and multiple geographies where available. Record sources and dates.

Stage 2 — Identify unmet needs ▼

Analyse negative reviews, recurring complaints, work-arounds customers invent, and gaps between stated expectations and delivered experience. Unmet needs are signals, not yet opportunities.

Stage 3 — Identify gaps ▼

Look systematically for price gaps, product-feature gaps, quality gaps, convenience gaps, experience gaps, distribution gaps and customer-segment gaps. Map which gaps are currently occupied by no credible player.

Stage 4 — Develop propositions ▼

Generate possible products, services, bundles or business-model variants that address the gaps. Keep each proposition concise and labelled as a hypothesis.

Stage 5 — Validate demand ▼

Use customer interviews, surveys, marketplace search data, competitor response analysis and, where feasible, prototype or concept tests. Demand validation reduces, but does not eliminate, uncertainty.

Stage 6 — Evaluate economics ▼

Assess product cost, distribution cost, marketing cost, achievable price, expected volume, repeat purchase behaviour, customer acquisition cost and resulting contribution margin. Reject propositions that cannot clear a realistic commercial hurdle even under optimistic assumptions.

Stage 7 — Prioritise opportunities ▼

Score remaining candidates on demand strength, market attractiveness, competitive intensity, differentiation potential, commercial viability, operational feasibility and scalability. Use the scoring matrix below.

Hypothetical opportunity examples

The following are illustrative hypotheses only. They are not proven market opportunities and require independent validation.

Hypothesis

Modular repair-friendly furniture

A mid-premium furniture line designed for easy part replacement and long service life, targeting customers frustrated by disposable furniture and limited repair options.

Hypothesis

Quiet productivity apparel

Workwear that prioritises acoustic comfort, temperature regulation and professional appearance for open-plan and hybrid work environments.

Hypothesis

Transparent-origin leather goods

A leather accessories brand that publishes full material and workshop provenance for customers who currently cannot verify claims of craftsmanship or ethics.

Hypothesis

Small-space hospitality kits

Curated, high-quality home hospitality products sized and designed for urban apartments with limited storage and serving space.

Hypothesis

Post-purchase education platform for complex durables

A service layer that helps owners of high-value products (watches, specialised equipment, vehicles) maintain, understand and get more value from ownership.
