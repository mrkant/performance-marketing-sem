360-Degree Marketing Framework

How products reach customers, and how channel choices shape brand perception, conversion and economics.

Channel types

Flagship and controlled retail, department stores, specialty retailers, mass retail, e-commerce, marketplaces, direct-to-consumer, wholesale, franchising, pop-ups and travel retail each carry different implications for brand control, margin, customer data and experience quality.

Key diagnostic themes

  • Store experience and visual merchandising
  • Product placement and conversion mechanics
  • Average transaction value and basket size
  • Footfall, traffic quality and conversion rate
  • Stock availability and inventory discipline
  • Channel conflict and grey-market risk
  • Omnichannel coherence

Distribution is both an access system and a brand medium. Selective distribution protects positioning; intensive distribution maximises reach at the risk of dilution. The right balance is category- and brand-specific.

See also the Distribution branch of the Fishbone Framework and the case studies of Hermès, UNIQLO, IKEA and Apple.
