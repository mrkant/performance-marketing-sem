A practical starting point

Six questions before serious spend

Before increasing budget or adding channels, these questions usually matter more than the channel itself:

  1. What is the real job of this money — new awareness, response from people who already know us, or both?
  2. Can the offer, website, language support and fulfilment absorb more attention without quality falling?
  3. Who benefits if we spend more in this channel? Does their incentive match our outcome?
  4. What part of the expected result is likely incremental, and what part may be existing demand taking credit?
  5. If early numbers look weak, what will we stop? If they look strong, how will we check they are not inflated?
  6. Is the promise consistent with the actual experience the customer will get?

If these cannot be answered with reasonable clarity, more media is rarely the first fix.
