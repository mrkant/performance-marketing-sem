The Battle Plan

Hover to reveal your tactical advantage.

1. Escape the Image War

Stop using supplier photos.

Execution

Rent a local Japanese model. Shoot detailed lifestyle images showing texture and fit.

2. The "Fit" Advantage

General sizing doesn't work here.

Execution

Provide a specific Japanese size guide (肩幅, 身幅, 着丈). Show the jacket on different body types.

3. Build a Real Brand

Shell trademarks are vulnerable.

Execution

Register a Japanese trademark (商標) to unlock Amazon Brand Registry and block copycats.

4. Quality Over Clones

Price wars kill margins.

Execution

Upgrade the material (e.g., real down). Good quality justifies a higher price point and better reviews.

5. SEO Localization

Machine translation fails.

Execution

Use natural Japanese keywords: "メンズ ダウンジャケット ファー付き 暖かい". Hire a native copywriter.

6. New Channels

Don't fight the red ocean.

Execution

List on Amazon Haul (the low-price platform) to capture price-sensitive buyers separately from your main brand.
