Generate keyword variations, long-tails, questions, intent groups, blog titles and content topics from a seed keyword. Fully browser-based. No invented search volumes, CPC or difficulty scores.

What this tool does and does not do

  • Does: Generate variations, long-tails, questions, alphabetical expansions, intent groups, blog titles, FAQ questions and topic ideas.
  • Does not: Provide real monthly search volume, CPC, keyword difficulty or competition scores.
  • Intent labels are heuristic (based on common modifier words), not from search engine data.
  • Privacy: Everything runs in your browser. Saved lists use localStorage only on this device.
  • Language packs beyond English can be added later without changing the architecture.
