Method & limitations

  • Uses a two-proportion z-test (pooled standard error) with two-tailed p-value.
  • Supports 90%, 95% and 99% confidence levels.
  • Sample-size estimates assume 80% power and the observed control rate as baseline.
  • Small samples and very low conversion counts trigger warnings — the normal approximation is less reliable.
  • Statistical significance is not the same as practical business significance. Always judge lift size against cost and risk.
  • Does not adjust for multiple comparisons or sequential testing (peeking).
