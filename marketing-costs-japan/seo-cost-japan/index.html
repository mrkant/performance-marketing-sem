SEO in Japan: costs and how it works

By Shashikant · Updated 19 November 2026 · How this hub is made and checked

⚡ In one minute

SEO consulting runs ¥100,000–500,000 a month and articles ¥25,000–50,000 each. Rankings usually move between months four and nine, so treat guaranteed positions with caution.

SEO builds organic traffic by improving a site's technical health, content and authority. It is slower than ads, but traffic does not stop when payment stops, so it suits businesses with a long horizon.

What it includes

Keyword research, competitor analysis, site structure and technical fixes (internal SEO), content planning and article production, internal linking, and, less often today, external link building. In 2026 many providers also advise on appearing in AI-generated search answers.

What it costs

Consulting typically costs ¥100,000–500,000 per month . Internal SEO fixes cost about ¥100,000–300,000 per month or ¥100,000–500,000 as a one-off. Content costs ¥25,000–50,000 per article . Large SEO firms usually start near ¥500,000 per month, while freelancers charge ¥50,000–300,000. A rule of thumb from price guides: ¥100,000–300,000 monthly buys limited help for small sites, ¥300,000–800,000 buys a standard programme for mid-sized companies, and ¥800,000–1.5 million or more buys aggressive work in competitive categories.

How it works

The first month is audit and strategy. Months two to four are fixes and content publication. Meaningful ranking gains typically show between month four and month nine, depending on the site's authority and competition. Anyone promising first-page rankings in weeks should be treated with caution.

Before you pay: what to ask and how to measure it

Ask which pages will be built or fixed, how many articles are included, and what is reported besides rankings. Rankings are not revenue, so ask for organic visits and conversions. Be wary of guaranteed positions, and confirm you keep ownership of all content and access.

Sources: published 2026 Japanese price guides (Imitsu, Meetsmore, Issoh, Atsoho, StockSun, Tatap, Pamxy, Ownly, Ecnomikata, NTT East). Ranges are indicative, last checked November 2026. This is an educational resource, not an offer of services.

SEO content is part of the brand’s public face. Thin, translated or purely keyword-driven pages can rank temporarily and still damage trust. Align SEO content with the actual product, service language and customer questions. Technical SEO that improves speed and mobile experience also improves the customer experience, not only the rankings.

Brand and experience implications

Track organic traffic, rankings for a defined term set, conversions from organic, and technical health metrics. Avoid judging a programme only by a single head term. Many valuable journeys start on longer, lower-volume queries. Attribute carefully: organic and paid often assist each other; last-click models can understate SEO’s contribution.

Measurement

SEO is slower than paid media. Meaningful movement in competitive categories often takes months, not weeks. Budgets that assume rapid ranking gains for difficult terms usually disappoint. Set expectations around leading indicators (indexation, crawl health, content output, ranking for supporting terms) as well as lagging ones (organic sessions and conversions).

Time horizon and expectations

SEO is a mix of technical health, content quality, and authority. Quotes that only mention “content” or only mention “links” are incomplete. A useful programme usually covers technical audits and fixes, content planning and production in Japanese, internal linking, measurement, and ongoing iteration. One-off projects can fix a clear technical problem; ongoing retainers make more sense when the competitive set is active.
