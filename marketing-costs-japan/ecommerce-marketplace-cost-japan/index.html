E-commerce and marketplace management in Japan: costs

By Shashikant · Updated 19 November 2026 · How this hub is made and checked

Online retail in Japan splits between marketplaces (Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Yahoo! Shopping) and brand-owned shops (often Shopify, BASE or EC-CUBE). Many brands sell through both.

Building a shop

Shopify or BASE builds typically cost ¥100,000–500,000 plus a small monthly platform fee. EC-CUBE and similar open-source builds run ¥1–3 million, and fully custom development starts around ¥3 million and is only justified for large operations.

Marketplace operation

Operation agencies handle product pages, images, pricing, campaigns, advertising, inventory coordination and reporting. Typical fees are ¥100,000–400,000 per month , or 10–20% of sales plus ¥50,000–150,000 fixed. Advisory-only contracts cost ¥50,000–500,000 per month depending on involvement. Each marketplace has its own culture: Rakuten rewards point campaigns and big sales events, Amazon rewards product page quality and advertising, and Yahoo! Shopping relies on campaign participation.

How it works

Onboarding and product setup take about a month, followed by ongoing optimisation. Sales events, such as seasonal sales, cause large swings, so plan inventory and budgets around the calendar.

Before you pay: what to ask and how to measure it

Separate platform fees, agency fees and ad spend in the quote. Ask who controls the shop account, product data and customer reviews, and how returns and customer messages are handled. Marketplace sales can rise while profit falls after fees and campaigns.

On a marketplace the platform owns much of the experience. Reviews, delivery speed and return handling become part of your brand whether you control them fully or not. Invest in listing quality, accurate inventory and responsive Japanese-language support. Advertising that drives traffic to a weak listing or slow fulfilment damages ratings and future organic performance.

Brand and experience implications

Marketplace dashboards report their own conversions well. They are weaker at showing the full customer journey or the value of a customer who later buys on your own site. Track both marketplace-reported numbers and any identifiable cross-channel behaviour. For brands with both marketplace and owned presence, lifetime value and repeat rate are usually more important than single-order ROAS on any one platform.

Measurement realities

A low commission rate can still be expensive if advertising and service costs are high.

  • Photography and content that meets each platform’s standards.
  • Advertising inside the marketplace (sponsored products, etc.).
  • Inventory and fulfilment decisions (FBA-style services versus self-fulfilment).
  • Customer-service labour in Japanese, including returns and messaging response times.
  • Periodic redesign or optimisation of listing content as algorithms and competitors change.

Costs that sit outside the fee schedule

Marketplaces (Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Yahoo! Shopping and others) give immediate traffic and trust signals at the cost of fees, ranking competition and limited control of the customer relationship. An own site or Shopify store gives more control and better data, but requires you to generate the traffic. Most brands that succeed in Japan use both, with clear roles: marketplaces for discovery and volume, owned channels for higher-margin repeat and brand control.
