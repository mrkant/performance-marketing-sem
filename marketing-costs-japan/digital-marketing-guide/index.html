Digital marketing in Japan: costs by service, 2026 overview

By Shashikant · Updated 19 November 2026 · How this hub is made and checked

⚡ In one minute

A one-page overview of digital costs by service, with links to the detailed guides.

A practical guide to agency types, pricing models and 2026 price ranges for ads, SEO, websites, social media, influencers, e-commerce and LINE, written for brands and founders who want to understand the Japanese market before they spend a yen.

Japan is the world's fourth-largest economy, a market where consumers search, shop and chat on platforms that look quite different from those in the United States or Europe. Yahoo! JAPAN still matters alongside Google. LINE is the default messaging app. Rakuten and Amazon compete head to head in e-commerce. X remains unusually popular. Agencies, contracts and invoices follow local conventions that surprise many foreign companies.

This guide explains how the market is organised, how agencies charge, what each service typically costs, and which Japan-specific rules and habits are worth knowing. All prices are indicative market ranges collected in November 2026 from published Japanese price guides and agency pages. Real quotes vary with scope, industry and season, and most figures exclude 10% consumption tax. Use the ranges to judge whether a quote is sensible, not as a price list.

The market in numbers

Dentsu's annual survey put total Japanese advertising spend in 2025 at about ¥8.06 trillion, up 5.1% on the year and the fourth consecutive record. Internet advertising reached roughly ¥4.05 trillion, up 10.8%, and for the first time accounted for more than half of all advertising spend (50.2%). Television, newspapers, magazines and radio together were close to flat.

Within internet advertising, media spend (the money paid to Google, Meta, Yahoo!, TikTok and similar platforms) was about ¥3.31 trillion, driven by video and especially vertical short-form video on social platforms. Dentsu and its partners forecast about 8% further growth in 2026. Internet ad production, meaning the creative work, was around ¥492 billion, helped by a rising number of video ads being made.

The practical lesson is that Japan is a large, growing and increasingly digital market, but also a mature one. Competition for search keywords and social attention is real, and local expectations about service quality are high.

Paid search and social advertising

Paid advertising in Japan means Google Ads, Yahoo! JAPAN Ads (now part of LY Corporation's advertising business), Meta (Facebook and Instagram), X, TikTok, LINE Ads, YouTube, and for retailers Amazon and Rakuten advertising. Search ads capture people who are already looking. Social and video ads create demand among people who are not.

Read the detailed guide: Paid search and social advertising

SEO (search engine optimisation)

SEO builds organic traffic by improving a site's technical health, content and authority. It is slower than ads, but traffic does not stop when payment stops, so it suits businesses with a long horizon.

Read the detailed guide: SEO (search engine optimisation)

Websites and landing pages

Prices vary more than almost anywhere else. Agencies commonly charge from ¥100,000 to over ¥5 million, freelancers ¥50,000–300,000, and template or subscription services a few thousand yen per month. At one large Japanese marketplace the average corporate site cost about ¥956,000 while the median was ¥500,000, because a few expensive projects pull the average up. Most small and mid-sized company sites land at ¥300,000–1,000,000 . Recruiting sites with entry forms and staff stories run ¥500,000–1.5 million.

Read the detailed guide: Websites and landing pages

Social media management

Social media agencies run company accounts on Instagram, X, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and LINE. By 2026 the work has moved beyond simple posting. Short video is now a standard part of many contracts, and the better providers connect organic content with advertising and e-commerce traffic.

Read the detailed guide: Social media management

Influencer marketing

Japanese influencer marketing covers Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, X and blogs, with a strong beauty, fashion, food, travel and lifestyle focus. Companies either hire creators directly, use a matching platform or hire a casting agency.

Read the detailed guide: Influencer marketing

Creative and video production

Creative is the biggest driver of results on social platforms, and production costs are rising with the volume of video advertising.

Read the detailed guide: Creative and video production

E-commerce and marketplaces

Online retail in Japan splits between marketplaces (Amazon, Rakuten Ichiba, Yahoo! Shopping) and brand-owned shops (often Shopify, BASE or EC-CUBE). Many brands sell through both.

Read the detailed guide: E-commerce and marketplaces

LINE official accounts and CRM

LINE is used by most people in Japan. Businesses use official accounts to send messages, coupons and reservation links, and to run automated step campaigns. It is particularly strong for retail, salons, clinics, restaurants and services.

Read the detailed guide: LINE official accounts and CRM
