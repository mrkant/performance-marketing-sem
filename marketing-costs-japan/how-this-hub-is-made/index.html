How this hub is made and checked

By Shashikant · Updated 19 November 2026 · How this hub is made and checked

⚡ In one minute

Sources, evidence labels, independence, and how corrections are handled.

This hub explains how advertising is sold in Japan and what published guides say it costs. Because readers make spending decisions from it, this page explains where the information comes from, how it is checked and what its limits are.

Independence

This is a personal educational project. It does not sell advertising or services, does not take payment from agencies or media owners, and does not receive fees for referrals. If that ever changes, the affected pages will say so clearly. Nothing here is professional, legal, tax or immigration advice.

Where the numbers come from

Prices and market figures come from published sources: Dentsu's annual advertising expenditure report, agency and media-owner pages, and Japanese price-comparison guides. Most price figures are ranges quoted by those sources, not negotiated rate cards, and each page lists its sources. Where sources disagree, the wider range is shown. Where no reliable figure was found, the page says so instead of estimating.

How claims are labelled

Verified means a primary or official source supports the statement. Published range means the figure comes from a published guide or agency listing. Interpretation is a judgement based on those facts. Illustrative, not a real company marks an invented teaching example. Not verified marks something that still needs checking.

The author

Published and maintained by Shashikant, a marketing professional based in Japan with about 20 years of experience across Japan and Northeast Asia. The views and edits are personal and do not represent any employer or client.

Updates and corrections

Every page shows when it was last updated. Prices and rules change, so figures should be rechecked about once a quarter. Corrections are welcome through the contact details on shashikant.jp, and material corrections will be noted on the page.

What this hub is not

It is not a rate card, a quote, a ranking of agencies or a recommendation of any company. Use it to understand scale and to ask better questions, then get written quotes from the providers themselves.
