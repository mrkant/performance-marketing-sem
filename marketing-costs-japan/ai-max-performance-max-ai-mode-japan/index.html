AI Max, Performance Max and ads in AI Mode in Japan: what is verified

By Shashikant · Updated 19 November 2026 · How this hub is made and checked

⚡ In one minute

AI Mode launched in Japanese on 9 September 2025 and AI Max for Search reached Japan as a beta in July 2025. Whether ads in AI Mode are live in Japan is not confirmed in the sources I found, so treat it as unverified.

Google's search advertising is moving toward automation. For advertisers in Japan the useful questions are what is available here, what is only announced elsewhere, and how to test without losing control of your claims. This page separates what is verified from what is not.

The three terms

Performance Max is a campaign type that serves ads across Google channels from a set of assets and goals. AI Max for Search adds AI-driven options to Search campaigns. AI Mode is Google's conversational search experience. Ads can appear there through existing Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns in supported markets.

What Google claims

Company-reported A trade report on Google Marketing Live 2026 says Google reported about 15% more conversions at similar return on ad spend, on average, for advertisers adopting AI Max or Performance Max. Google also cautions that results vary. Treat this as a vendor claim to test, not a forecast.

How ads enter AI Mode

Published guidance According to reporting on Google's help pages, eligible Search, Shopping and Performance Max campaigns enter the auction without a separate budget, advertisers cannot target AI Mode alone or opt out separately from Search, and some ads may show AI-generated supplementary descriptions. Check Google Ads Help or your account representative for the current position in Japan.

Protecting your claims

Interpretation When automation generates headlines or descriptions, you remain the advertiser. For cosmetics, supplements and health products in particular, review generated text against Japanese labelling and advertising rules, use the available controls, and keep a record of approved wording.

A safe way to test

Run any new setting as a controlled experiment: one budget, one clear goal, a fixed period, and the old setup kept as a comparison. Review the search terms report weekly, add brand and negative exclusions, and judge the result on profit after fees, not on platform-reported conversions alone.

Questions to ask an agency

Which of these features are switched on in my account, and who decided? What did the experiment compare? How are brand terms protected? Who reviews generated text for compliance? What reporting will show AI-driven results separately?
