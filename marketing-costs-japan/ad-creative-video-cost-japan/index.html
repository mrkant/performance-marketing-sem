Ad creative and video production costs in Japan

By Shashikant · Updated 19 November 2026 · How this hub is made and checked

⚡ In one minute

Video ads range from about ¥30,000 to over ¥1 million. Agree versions, usage period for talent and music, and file ownership before you start.

Creative is the biggest driver of results on social platforms, and production costs are rising with the volume of video advertising.

What it costs

Simple banner ads start around ¥1,500 each from freelancers, with agency pricing considerably higher. Video ads range widely, from about ¥30,000 for simple editing to ¥1 million or more for productions with casting, shooting and direction. Many brands now order packages of several short vertical videos per month so that they can test variations.

How it works

Brief, concept, script or storyboard, production, revisions and delivery in formats for each platform. Agree in advance who owns the footage, how long you may use any models or music, and how many versions are included.

Before you pay: what to ask and how to measure it

Agree the number of versions, formats, revision rounds, usage period for talent and music, and who owns the footage. Cheap creative that is never tested wastes media spend, so plan to test several versions and record which one works.

Sources: published 2026 Japanese price guides (Imitsu, Meetsmore, Issoh, Atsoho, StockSun, Tatap, Pamxy, Ownly, Ecnomikata, NTT East). Ranges are indicative, last checked November 2026. This is an educational resource, not an offer of services.

  • Ordering one expensive hero video and then running it unchanged for months.
  • Using the same asset across every platform without adaptation (vertical, captions, length and first three seconds matter).
  • Ignoring Japanese-language nuance and cultural cues in scripts and talent choice.
  • Failing to secure usage rights that match the planned flight length and media mix.

Common pitfalls

Creative sets the expectation the customer brings to the product or service. If the video promises a calm, premium experience and the website or packaging feels chaotic, the mismatch costs trust. Align creative direction with the actual customer journey, including support language and fulfilment quality. Advertising that over-promises relative to the experience creates short-term clicks and longer-term scepticism.

Brand and experience implications

Track performance by creative variant, not only by campaign. Record which hook, length, caption style and visual treatment drove the best results. Keep a simple library of what worked. Many brands run the same under-performing creative for months because no one is looking at creative-level data. When media spend is large, a structured creative testing plan (new variants every two to four weeks) is usually worth more than another media optimisation tweak.

How to measure creative, not only media

A video package is rarely just “a film”. It usually includes concept, scripting or storyboard, talent or voice, music or licensed tracks, shooting or motion design, editing, captions, platform-specific cuts, and a defined number of revision rounds. Usage rights for talent and music are often time-limited. Clarify all of these before work starts. Cheap quotes sometimes omit usage rights or revisions and then charge for them later.

What you are actually buying

On social and many digital platforms, the creative is the targeting. Weak creative is rejected by the user before the algorithm has a chance to optimise. Strong creative expands the effective audience and improves cost efficiency. This is why production budgets that look high relative to media spend can still be rational: they raise the return on every yen of media.
